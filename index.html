How to Enable Developer Options:

  • Step 1: On your phone, go to settings
  • Step 2: Tap "About device" then go search how to enable Developer options on (Your Device)

How to Use Wireless Mode:

  • Step 1: On your phone, go to Developer Options and open the Wireless Debugging menu.
  • Step 2: Tap "Pair device with pairing code" to see your pairing IP, Port, and 6-digit code.
  • Step 3: Paste that pairing IP and Port into the terminal when prompted.
  • Step 4: Look at the main screen of Wireless Debugging for the new connection port, paste it in, and enjoy!
